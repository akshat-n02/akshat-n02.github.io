Audio Technician - John Hopkins 

Following my internship I was invited to help set up production for the John Hopkins showcase as Funkhaus. The event took place on the 14th of February 2026 and beforehand I was responsible for setting up 112 point sound system in the performance space. This sound system was not only used by John Hopkins but Ruthless and William Russel as well. 

Spatial Festival
During my internship a major focus throughout was preparing for SPATIAL festival held at Funkhaus in September 12, 13 and 14th. SPATIAL festival is a celebration of spatial sound technologies as a form of contemporary art. Preparing for the festival my responsibilities included building the sets in Saal 1, Saal 2, and at MONOM. 

Saal 1
Built a 72 point sound system, responsible for directing and explaining volunteers and fellow trainees the correct rigging, cabling, and setup of the specific sound system. I additionally did soundcehck with Artists. During the festival I was responsible for stage buildup and set down after acts. 

Saal 2 
Set up installation Video Barnum X MONOM. Video Barnum was an installation perceived by Pierre Paulin in 1985, originally envisioned to be an immersive environment integrating architecture, object and image. However, MONOM replaced the visual focus with Spatial Audio architecture. Our team setup 60 point sound system as well all building up the objects of the installation from Paulin, Paulin, Paulin.

MONOM 
Produced the space and was responsible for the event operations in this room throughout the festival. This included escorting the audience in and out of the venue, maintaining event schedule, organizing lights and playback of spatial sound pieces. Was also the on board technician in case of any speaker malfunctions or technical difficulties. 

Throughout this festival I played a leading role in guiding volunteers, assigning them tasks during the event, setting up microphoning, maintaining scheduled event operations, soundcheck, and dealing with technical difficulties. I was also responsible for the afterparty at MONOM Bar organizing the sound system and dj setup. Many last second problems came out during this festival however I was able to deal with many of them often times with creative thinking and calm vibe.
