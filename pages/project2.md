4DSOUND specializes in innovative spatial sound technology, creating immersive audio environments for artistic, cultural, and commercial applications. My role combined administrative tasks alongside marketing and outreach to promote, improve, and grow the 4DSOUND software.  

Pitch and Client Communications
Independently designed and delivered presentation decks used directly by the founds for prospective and existing client meetings. The purpose was to translate complex Spatial Audio concepts into a clear narrative for commercial audiences. This included explaining the technology, use case applications, and their existing network. The created deck can be viewed here. Other variations of this deck were made depending on context of the pitch and audience.

Artist Outreach and Customer Research
Led outreach and organized research interviews with high-profile artists (including Mac DeMarco and Grand River) who had already used the software. The goal of these interviews were to collect feedback and understand the strengths and shortcomings of the software from artists who have interacted with the software.

Website Improvements
Seeing how there were informational gaps across the company website, I took the initiative to update the projects section (link) of the 4DSOUND website. 

Software Licensing
Acted as primary point of contact for 4DSOUND software licensing process, reviewing and filtering licensing requests and keeping na up to date record of all activities on the company database

Archival Database Redesign 
I redesigned and organized the 4DSOUND archival database which documented all artists (100+) use of the 4DSOUND software. The database was organized to easily implement new data and make sure all 4DSOUND projects were standardized ensuring consistent information about project details, recordings, dates, locations, credits.
